Aug 21 – 24 · Interior

Denali

Denali National Park and Preserve covers more than 6 million acres around North America's highest peak: sweeping tundra, low taiga forest, and glacier-carved mountains. It is known for wildlife viewing, including Alaska's "Big Five": grizzly bears, wolves, moose, caribou, and Dall sheep.

Top activities

Wildlife & park tours
Transit or narrated tour along the 92-mile Denali Park Road. The Pretty Rocks bridge opened Sept 2026; bus service to Kantishna (mile 92) is expected to resume June 8, 2027.Reserve Denali (park buses)
Hiking
Easy: Horseshoe Lake Trail. Hard and worth it: Mount Healy Overlook Trail.NPS: plan your visit
Flightseeing
Small plane or helicopter over the glaciers, with glacier-landing options.Denali Air (park entrance, fixed-wing) · TEMSCO Helicopters (glacier landing, heli-hike) · K2 Aviation (Talkeetna, summit flights)
Sled dog kennels
The park's working NPS sled dogs, with demonstrations. Free; ride the park shuttle.NPS: plan your visit
Nenana River rafting
Whitewater or calmer scenic floats along the park's edge.Denali Raft Adventures (4.9★, 1,500+ Tripadvisor reviews; 11-mile Wilderness Run scenic float, ~3 h)

Places to stay

Option 1

Grande Denali Lodge

High on Sugarloaf Mountain, minutes from the park entrance. Sweeping valley views, alpine-style rooms, central location.

Highlight: dinner at the Alpenglow Restaurant with panoramic views.

Option 2

Denali Princess Wilderness Lodge

Full-service resort-style lodge about a mile from the park entrance (Mile 238.5 Parks Hwy). Rustic-chic décor, King Salmon Restaurant, Grizzly Burger, a café, and an hourly shuttle to the park.

Highlight: the big outdoor deck over the Nenana River canyon.

Option 3

Denali Bluffs Hotel

Set into the hillside over the Nenana River valley. Classic Alaskan lodge feel, close to the park entrance and excursion pickups. Same owner as Grande Denali.

Highlight: on-site Mountaineer Bar & Grill and Perky Moose Café.

Restaurant listed elsewhere as Mountaineer Bar & Grill, not "Apex." Confirm with the hotel.
Aug 24 – 27 · Coast & marine

Kenai Fjords

Where ocean, ice, and coastal mountains meet: tidewater glaciers calving into the sea, sheltered coves, coastal rainforest, and marine wildlife including orcas, humpback whales, sea otters, and puffins.

Getting there and pacing

Transit from Anchorage
About 4.25 hours on the Alaska Railroad Coastal Classic in GoldStar Service (glass-domed ceilings, upper outdoor viewing platform), or about 2.5 hours down the Seward Highway along Turnagain Arm. On this trip you fly in from Denali and take the train back.
Pacing
Flexible. Easy moves between town, rail, and coastal trailheads.
Activities
Full-day glacier and wildlife cruises, the 8-mile round-trip Harding Icefield Trail from Exit Glacier, sea kayaking, and salmon fishing charters.Kenai Fjords Tours (cruises; Fox Island lodge operator) · Major Marine Tours (full-day Northwestern Fjord cruise) · Sunny Cove Sea Kayaking (Aialik Bay glacier kayak + cruise, ~$445) · Exit Glacier Guides (guided Harding Icefield hike, $139; Exit Glacier ice hike) · Miller's Landing (family-run salmon charters, 40+ years)

Basecamps

Island lodge

Kenai Fjords Wilderness Lodge

Fox Island in Resurrection Bay, about 12 miles south of Seward and reachable only by boat. Meals included, with naturalist hikes, kayaking, and yoga. Isolated, with historic charm.

2027 season Jun 2 – Sep 11. August showed as available; July and September sold out.

Standard package is 2 nights (4 nights on select dates). Ask about 3 nights. 25% off 2027 stays booked by Jan 15, 2027.
Mainland

Seward Windsong Lodge

Mainland comfort near Exit Glacier and the Harding Icefield trailhead, a short drive to the Seward harbor for cruises and fishing.

Different bay

Tutka Bay Lodge

Luxury fjord lodge with guided kayaking, hiking, halibut and salmon fishing, and a daily cooking class. From $9,900 for 3 nights including meals, guide, excursions, and house wine and beer. 3-night minimum.

Not in Seward. It's on Kachemak Bay across from Homer, about 5 hours by road from Anchorage. Access is a ~60-minute floatplane from Anchorage, or a flight to Homer plus a 30-minute boat. Choosing it means a Denali → Tutka Bay charter instead of Seward, and no Coastal Classic home.

Open Items

  1. Anchorage night 1: book from Aug 19 for the 12:53 AM arrival.
  2. MSP connection: 32 minutes is tight on one ticket and fails off one. Look for 60+ minutes.
  3. Return routing: the 1:15 PM departure carries an overnight; compare an ANC red-eye that lands mid-morning.
  4. Denali → Seward charter: Denali Air does flightseeing only, not point-to-point. Kantishna Air Taxi flies direct to Seward but its 2026–27 operating status is unconfirmed. Call them and line up a second charter as backup.
  5. Kenai Fjords lodge: pick Wilderness Lodge vs. Windsong vs. Tutka Bay. Wilderness Lodge sells 2-night packages, so ask about 3 nights, and book by Jan 15, 2027 for 25% off.
  6. Denali lodging: rooms are tight. Hold whichever of Grande Denali, Princess, or Bluffs has Aug 21–24 now, with free cancellation.
  7. Park road: book a Kantishna or long transit bus in the 2027 reservation system when it opens.
  8. Rail: book GoldStar on both the Denali Star and the Coastal Classic; summer seats sell out early.
